Geographic Expansion & Site Selection

Snow Pack · Payments · Go-to-market

Your next market isn't the biggest one. Find where demand is growing and you're barely there.

A merchant acquirer's expansion team has a budget for a few new markets. The obvious pick is the largest pool of volume, but a big pool you already cover, or a category that isn't growing, is a poor bet. This example ranks every metro × merchant-category cell by opportunity score = demand growth × coverage gap on a governed semantic view, puts it on a map, and has Cortex AI_COMPLETE write the expansion note. A second AI pass checks every figure and refuses anything unsupported, hedged or forecast.

Synthetic data, illustrative logic. Corvane Payments is fictional, and every metro's sizing and coverage figure is invented; only the city names and their coordinates are real geography. This is an example, not a production system and not investment advice.

What you get

Overview

White space, penetration and the top target as KPI tiles, a metro × category heat map of the opportunity score, and 24 months of category demand.

Market map

A dot-map of the US with each metro sized by white-space dollars, region totals from the semantic view, and a panel per metro.

Opportunity ranking

All 60 cells on a penetration vs growth scatter and in a sortable, filterable table with Top target, Saturated and No-growth pills.

Verified notes and Q&A

An expansion note that cites its fact pack and gets a Verified stamp, and a Cortex Agent that answers in plain English and shows its SQL.

opportunity_score = max(category demand growth %, 0) × (1 − penetration). It is an index, not dollars, which is why the largest white-space pool (New York grocery, $17.53B) ranks 7th.

Run it in your account

  1. Run sql/setup.sql in a Snowsight worksheet (about 15 s).
  2. Upload the app/ files to the stage and run sql/deploy_streamlit.sql, or run the app locally in Live mode with key-pair auth.
  3. Optional: get the free Financial Data Package and run sql/setup_census.sql for real category demand.
  4. Run sql/cleanup.sql to remove the database, warehouse and role.
